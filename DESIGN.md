# Direction: A place to grow

Persuade surface: learner recognition → founder → practice → community → evidence → fit → enquiry.

## Visual world

An independent education journal, expressed through expansive forest-green fields, ivory paper, lime annotations and candid photography. DM Sans display/body, Cormorant Garamond italic for human emphasis. Thin rules, crisp geometry and large breathing spaces. Avoid dashboard decoration and fabricated authority.

## First viewport

A full-bleed alpine landscape from comp1 with four registered layers. “Find your perspective.” sits between the mountains and foreground hiker. Native scroll drives background, mountain, headline and foreground at 70/55/40/10 percent rates. The lower green fade keeps educational context and the programme action legible. These are supplied demo photographs, pending approved replacement imagery.

## Signature interactions

Layered hero adapted from comp1 using existing Motion, scroll-responsive community photography from comp4, an elastic gallery from comp2 and programme image previews from comp6. Native scrolling; no wheel trapping. Reduced-motion preference removes scroll transforms and transitions. Content remains visible without animation.

## Component reference decisions

Use comp1/comp2/comp4/comp6 as adapted starting points. comp3's scroll trapping and comp5's cartoon crowd are omitted.

## Tokens

Paper #f5f3ea; ink #193d31; forest #173c30; deep #102b23; lime #d7ef8f; muted #59685e; rule #ced3c4; white #fffef7. DM Sans Variable for body/display and Cormorant Garamond italic for emphasis. Section padding 112px / 5vw desktop, 70px / 24px mobile. Controls have 4px radii. Primary motion easing cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).

## Build contract

Responsive Next.js site with server-rendered copy, four detail routes, working navigation, programme enquiry selection, mixed text/portrait-video testimonial layouts, accessible preview dialog, FAQ and team. No price, media milestone or result invented. Local real team images and labeled stock community photography. Preview noindex by default; enable indexing only on publication.

Impeccable launcher context and concept-seed were attempted but engine is unavailable. Manual code-led execution follows its craft guidance. User authorized implementation after discovery and supplied content corrections; no additional design gate imposed.
