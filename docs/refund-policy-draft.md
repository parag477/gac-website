# Proposed cancellation and refund policy

**Owner review required before publication.** This draft is not linked from the public website and is not an active customer policy. Green Arc Commune must confirm it can honour these terms. No payment-provider processing time or refund deadline has been invented.

## Before learning begins

You may request a full refund before your first paid teaching session begins, provided access to programme recordings or other paid learning materials has not started. Email support@greenarccommune.com with your enrolment name, programme and payment reference. Do not send card details, bank passwords or other sensitive financial information.

## Duplicate payments

An accidental duplicate payment for the same enrolment will be refunded after the team verifies the duplicate.

## If Green Arc Commune cancels

If Green Arc Commune cancels a programme before delivery begins, you may request a full refund. If the programme is cancelled after partial delivery, the undelivered portion will be refunded. The team will explain how that portion is calculated.

## After teaching or material access begins

Refund requests made after the first paid session or after access to paid recordings or learning materials begins will be reviewed individually. Approval is not guaranteed. Explain the circumstances in your email so the team can review the request.

## Requests and decisions

Send requests to support@greenarccommune.com. Green Arc Commune will confirm the decision and applicable refund arrangements in writing. Refunds do not restrict any consumer rights that apply by law.

## Details to confirm before publication

- Approval of the policy above.
- Payment method, operational response/refund timeframe, rescheduling and missed-session rules.
- Each programme’s duration, schedule, recording access and access period.
- Business contact/address information required for the business’s actual operations.

This is proposed business copy, not a determination that it satisfies every legal obligation. The website’s factual education/disclosure pages should not present it as agreed until approved.
